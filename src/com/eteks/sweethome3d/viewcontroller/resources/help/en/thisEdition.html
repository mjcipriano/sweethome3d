Sweet Home 3D Help
  What this edition adds
 

This edition of Sweet Home 3D adds the features below to the ones described in the other pages of this help. The complete guides, with pictures, are at github.com/mjcipriano/sweethome3d.

Artificial intelligence

•

Help > Claude MCP server... lets Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP client of this computer read the home, look at pictures of the plan and of the 3D view, and edit the home. Click Start server, then Connect Claude Desktop or Connect Claude Code, once. Each change Claude makes is one step of Edit > Undo. Untick Allow changes to this home to let Claude read only.

•

Help > Design assistant... opens a chat that answers questions about the home and edits it. Choose your AI service and your key with Settings.... Help > What can I ask the assistant? lists examples.

•

Both can build a whole house: roofs over any shape with hips, gables and dormers, walls cut to the slope of a roof, slabs and ramps, and a ground with slopes.

Plan

•

Plan > Add room from shape... adds a rectangle, L, T or U shaped room with its walls.

•

Plan > Apply style to rooms... colors the floor, the ceiling and the walls of the selected rooms in a decoration style.

•

Plan > Add room dimensions adds the dimension lines that give the size of each room.

•

Plan > Home report and budget... gives floor areas, wall surfaces and furniture counts, and a budget from unit prices.

•

Plan > Export elevation drawing... writes a drawing of the home seen from one of its sides.

•

File > Versions... keeps named copies of the home beside its file and compares them.

Furniture

•

Right click in the furniture list or in the plan, then Tags, to give tags to furniture. Furniture > Layers and tags... shows and hides at once the furniture of a tag or of a type.

•

Furniture > Replace by the piece selected in catalog swaps the selected furniture for the piece selected in the catalog, at the same place.

3D view

•

The mouse wheel, the keys + and -, and the 3D +, 3D − and 3D fit buttons zoom the 3D view. The keys 1 to 9 in the 3D view, or 3D views in the tool bar, show the house from its front (2), its back (8), its left (4), its right (6), its corners (1, 3, 7, 9) and the top (5); Shift looks from higher. The keys Q and E, or Alt with a drag of the mouse, move the camera sideways.

•

3D view > Hide walls in front of the camera (doll house) leaves out the walls between the camera and the rooms.

•

3D view > Use OpenGL renderer (experimental) draws the 3D view faster in large homes; 3D view > OpenGL renderer quality adds shadows and soft shading.

•

3D view > Generate model LOD cache prepares lighter versions of heavy furniture models, then shown by 3D view > Reduce detail of heavy models automatically.

•

3D view > Export 360° panorama... and 3D view > Export to Blender... prepare a panorama to look around in, and a scene for a high quality picture.

Visiting the home

•

Tools > WebXR Preview... shows the home at life size in a Meta Quest or SteamVR headset, from the browser of the headset.

•

Tools > Game mode... opens the home in the browser of this computer: walk, jump, climb, fly, open the doors.

•

In both, the key N pins a note on what you look at. Tools > Review notes... lists the notes, and Claude can read them and do what they ask.

•

Tools > Export 3D web page... writes a folder that anyone can open in a browser, without Sweet Home 3D.
