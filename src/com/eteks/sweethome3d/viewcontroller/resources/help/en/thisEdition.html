Sweet Home 3D Help
  What this edition adds
 

This edition of Sweet Home 3D adds the features below to the ones described in the other pages of this help. The complete guides, with pictures, are at github.com/mjcipriano/sweethome3d.

Artificial intelligence

•

Tools > Claude MCP server... lets Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP client of this computer read the home, look at pictures of the plan and of the 3D view, and edit the home. Click Start server, then Connect Claude Desktop or Connect Claude Code, once. Each change Claude makes is one step of Edit > Undo. Untick Allow changes to this home to let Claude read only.

•

Tools > Design assistant... opens a chat that answers questions about the home and edits it. Choose your AI service and your key with Settings.... Tools > What can I ask the assistant? lists examples.

•

Both can build a whole house: roofs over any shape with hips, gables and dormers, walls cut to the slope of a roof, ceilings that follow it, slabs and ramps, a ground with slopes, and the lot with its property lines and setbacks.

•

Claude can also save versions of the home, compare and restore them, work on a copy, export the home, and see what you changed by hand. It writes the file of your home only if you tick Allow saving this home in its file, and replacing files in the window of the MCP server.

Plan

•

Plan > Add room from shape... adds a rectangle, L, T or U shaped room with its walls.

•

Plan > Apply style to rooms... colors the floor, the ceiling and the walls of the selected rooms in a decoration style.

•

Plan > Add room dimensions adds the dimension lines that give the size of each room.

•

Plan > Garden builds a fence, a hedge, a path or a planting bed along the line you drew with Plan > Create polylines and selected.

•

Plan > Electrical and plumbing symbols adds outlets, switches, lights and plumbing symbols, on the selected wall or along the walls of the selected rooms.

•

Plan > Measure the selection gives the free space around a piece of furniture, or the distance between two items.

•

Plan > Modify groups what modifies walls, rooms, lines, dimensions and texts.

File

•

File > Versions... keeps named copies of the home beside its file, compares them, and restores one in the home with Restore in this home.

•

File > Export holds every export: the plan (SVG), the furniture list (CSV), the 3D model (OBJ), Export elevation drawing... and Export elevation picture... for a side of the building with its dimensions, Export 360° panorama..., Export to Blender... and Export 3D web page..., a folder that anyone can open in a browser.

Furniture

•

Right click in the furniture list or in the plan, then Tags, to give tags to furniture. Furniture > Layers and tags... shows and hides at once the furniture of a tag or of a type.

•

Furniture > Replace by the piece selected in catalog swaps the selected furniture for the piece selected in the catalog, at the same place.

•

Furniture > Open the selected doors and windows... turns their leaves by an angle, and Close the selected doors and windows shuts them. Only the models made with hinges open.

•

Furniture > Furnish the selected room... places catalog furniture for a kind of room, clear of doors, windows and passages: a starting point to arrange by hand.

•

Furniture > Align and distribute and Furniture > Import group the alignments and the imports.

3D view

•

The mouse wheel, the keys + and -, and the 3D +, 3D − and 3D fit buttons zoom the 3D view. The keys 1 to 9 in the 3D view, or 3D views in the tool bar, show the house from its front (2), its back (8), its left (4), its right (6), its corners (1, 3, 7, 9) and the top (5); Shift looks from higher. The keys Q and E, or Alt with a drag of the mouse, move the camera sideways.

•

3D view > Hide walls in front of the camera (doll house) leaves out the walls between the camera and the rooms.

•

3D view > Use OpenGL renderer (experimental) draws the 3D view faster in large homes; 3D view > OpenGL renderer quality adds shadows and soft shading.

•

3D view > Generate model LOD cache prepares lighter versions of heavy furniture models, then shown by 3D view > Reduce detail of heavy models automatically.

•

3D view > Sun at the date and time of the camera lights and shadows the 3D view from where the sun is; 3D view > Date and time of the sun... chooses them, at the place of the compass.

Tools

•

Tools > Check the plan... lists the defects of the plan (walls that don't meet, doors out of walls, walls beyond a setback...) and fixes the ones that can be.

•

Tools > Home report and budget... gives floor areas, wall surfaces without their door and window openings, furniture counts, and a budget from unit prices.

•

Tools > Schedules lists doors and windows, rooms, furniture and symbols as tables to export in CSV, and Tools > Framing estimate... counts studs, plates and headers for a budget.

Visiting the home

•

Tools > WebXR Preview... shows the home at life size in a Meta Quest or SteamVR headset, from the browser of the headset.

•

Tools > Game mode... opens the home in the browser of this computer: walk, jump, climb, fly, open the doors.

•

In both, the key N pins a note on what you look at. Tools > Review notes... lists the notes, and Claude can read them and do what they ask.
